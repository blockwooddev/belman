# belman

A utility for mapping locations and context information for places to place fliers. 

## History

A town crier, or "bellman" or "belman" was an office of a royal court or public authority who makes public pronouncements. This tool is a way for those of us who desire to notify the public of community events to find places that are public forums-- coffeeshops, barbershops, libraries, etc. to place all manner of fliers, posters, postcards, broadsheets, etc.
